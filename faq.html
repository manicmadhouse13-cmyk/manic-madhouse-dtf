DTF Transfers & General Questions

Here are some of the questions we get asked most often.

What are DTF Transfers?

DTF (Direct to Film) transfers are high-quality printed designs that can be heat pressed onto a wide variety of fabrics, producing vibrant colours, fine detail and excellent durability.

What fabrics can DTF be applied to?

DTF transfers work on cotton, polyester, cotton blends, hoodies, workwear, tote bags and many other fabric types.

Do I need to order in bulk?

No. Whether you need a single custom shirt or a large business order, we're happy to help.

How long do orders take?

Turnaround times depend on the size of your order, but we always aim to complete and dispatch jobs as quickly as possible while maintaining premium quality.

Do you ship Australia wide?

Yes! We proudly ship our products right across Australia.

Custom Printing & Artwork

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

Can you print my own design?

Absolutely! We can print your supplied artwork, logo or custom design. If you're unsure whether your artwork is suitable, send it through and we'll happily check it for you before printing.

What artwork files do you accept?

We recommend high-resolution PNG files with transparent backgrounds where possible. We also accept PDF, SVG, AI, EPS and other common design formats for the best print quality.

Do you design logos or artwork?

Yes. If you don't already have artwork, we can discuss creating or refining a design to suit your business, event or personal project.

Can you print for businesses?

Definitely. We supply branded apparel, uniforms, promotional merchandise, stickers and other custom printing solutions for businesses of all sizes.

Do you offer wedding printing?

Yes! We create custom wedding signage, welcome boards, seating charts, apparel, gifts and personalised items to help make your special day memorable.

Can I request a custom quote?

Of course. Every project is different, so we're happy to provide personalised quotes based on your design, quantity and printing requirements.
