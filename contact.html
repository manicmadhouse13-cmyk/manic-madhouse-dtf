Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before requesting a quote.

How long do orders take?

Most jobs are completed within a few business days depending on artwork approval and order size.

Can I supply my own artwork?

Yes. We accept PNG, PDF, AI, EPS and SVG files.

Do you ship Australia wide?

Absolutely. Every order is shipped with tracking anywhere in Australia.

Can you create artwork for me?

Yes. If you don't have artwork yet, we'll help bring your idea to life.

Why don't you show prices?

Every order is customised, so we provide personalised quotes based on your requirements.

Can businesses order in bulk?

Yes. We regularly supply businesses, sports clubs, schools and events with bulk printing.
