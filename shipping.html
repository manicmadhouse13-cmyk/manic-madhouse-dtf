📦 SHIPPING INFORMATION

Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about order processing, Australia-wide delivery, tracking, returns and replacement requests.

Order Processing

Every order at Manic Madhouse DTF Designs is prepared specifically for each customer. Once your quote has been approved and payment arrangements have been confirmed, your order will move into production.

Production times vary depending on the size and complexity of your order, but we always aim to complete every project as quickly as possible while maintaining our high quality standards.

Shipping Australia Wide

We proudly ship throughout Australia using trusted courier and postal services. Delivery times may vary depending on your location and the selected shipping provider.

  • ✔ Australia-wide delivery available.
  • ✔ Orders are securely packaged before dispatch.
  • ✔ Tracking is provided where available.
  • ✔ FREE shipping on qualifying orders.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking details will be provided whenever available so you can monitor your delivery from dispatch through to arrival.

Returns & Exchanges

As the majority of our products are custom made and printed to your specifications, we generally cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind. Please ensure all artwork, garment selections, colours, sizes and quantities are correct before approving your quote.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or you believe an item has been produced incorrectly, please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order. Include photos of the issue so we can investigate and work with you to provide a fair resolution.

Lost or Delayed Shipments

Once an order has been handed to the shipping provider, delivery times are outside our direct control. If your parcel appears to be significantly delayed or lost, we'll gladly assist in lodging enquiries with the carrier and help track your order.

Need Assistance?

Our goal is to provide excellent customer service from your first enquiry through to delivery. If you have any questions about your order, shipping or returns, we're always happy to help.

Need Help With Your Order?

If you have any questions about shipping, delivery, returns or your order, we're here to help. Get in touch and we'll be happy to assist.
