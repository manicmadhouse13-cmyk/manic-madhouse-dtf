Privacy Policy

At Manic Madhouse DTF Designs, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we keep it secure.

Information We Collect

When you contact Manic Madhouse DTF Designs or request a quote, we may collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, business name (if applicable), shipping address and any artwork or files you choose to provide.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our products, prepare quotes, fulfil orders and communicate with you about your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • ✔ Prepare and provide personalised quotes.
  • ✔ Process and complete your orders.
  • ✔ Respond to enquiries and customer support requests.
  • ✔ Improve our products and services.
  • ✔ Contact you regarding your order when required.

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. While no online system can guarantee absolute security, we strive to keep your information safe using appropriate security practices.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences and help us understand how visitors use our website. Cookies do not give us access to your computer or any personal information beyond what you choose to provide.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as website hosting, analytics, email providers and shipping companies to help operate our business. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services.

Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct or remove your information where permitted by law. To make a request, simply contact us using the details below.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with the revised effective date.

Questions About Your Privacy?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please get in touch. We're happy to help.
