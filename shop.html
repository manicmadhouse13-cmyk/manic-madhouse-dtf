Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

What is a DTF Transfer?

DTF (Direct To Film) transfers are premium full-colour heat transfers suitable for cotton, polyester and blended fabrics.

Do you ship Australia wide?

Yes. Every order is shipped with tracked delivery anywhere in Australia.

Can I upload my own artwork?

Absolutely! Upload your artwork using our Design Your Own section and we'll prepare it for printing.

Do you make custom stickers?

Yes! We produce premium UV DTF stickers, business branding, labels and decals.

Can you print wedding signage?

Yes. We produce welcome signs, seating charts, acrylic signage, menus, place cards and more.

How do I request a quote?

Simply add products to your Quote List, then submit your request through our contact form.
