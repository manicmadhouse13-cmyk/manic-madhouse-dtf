Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

What is a DTF Transfer?

DTF (Direct To Film) transfers are full-colour heat transfers that can be applied to cotton, polyester and blended fabrics using a heat press.

Do you ship Australia wide?

Yes! We ship our products Australia-wide with tracked shipping.

Can I upload my own artwork?

Absolutely! Upload your artwork through our Design Your Own page and we'll prepare it for printing.

Do you make custom stickers?

Yes. We create premium custom stickers for businesses, events, vehicles, packaging and more.

Can you print wedding signage?

Yes! We create welcome signs, seating charts, bar menus, place cards and personalised wedding stationery.

How do I get a quote?

Browse our designs or upload your own artwork, then complete the Request a Quote form. We'll contact you with a personalised quote.
